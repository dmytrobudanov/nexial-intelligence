How it works

From signup to a ranked watchlist in three steps.

  1. 01

    Pick your sector

    Choose a sector and region, the markers you care about and how often you want the digest. About 2 minutes, no card.

  2. 02

    Get ranked markers with evidence

    Companies are ranked by marker strength and freshness. Every marker shows its sources, dates and a confidence level: Confirmed, Emerging or Hypothesis.

  3. 03

    Act

    Add companies to your watchlist, get a weekly or daily digest, export CSV, order a sector report or scope a pilot.

Grounding rule

Every marker needs 2+ independent, verifiable data points. Confidence is shown next to each one: Confirmed, Emerging or Hypothesis.
